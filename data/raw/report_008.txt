CEA and CA 19-9 not reported (results pending at time of surgery). No pre-operative staging CT
of chest available. Referred urgently for right hemicolectomy due to near-obstructing lesion.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received: Right hemicolectomy specimen, 35 cm in length including terminal ileum (8 cm) and
right colon (27 cm). A fungating, partially obstructing tumor measuring 5.5 x 4.8 x 2.1 cm is
identified at the cecum, 3 cm from the ileocecal valve. Tumor grossly penetrates through the
bowel wall. Mesenteric fat contains multiple lymph nodes, 12 identified. Appendix is present
and unremarkable.

Sections show moderately to poorly differentiated (Grade 2-3, mixed) colorectal adenocarcinoma.
