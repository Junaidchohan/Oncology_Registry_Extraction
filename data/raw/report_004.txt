CLINICAL HISTORY:
44-year-old female with incidentally discovered right breast mass on screening mammography, BI-RADS 4C.
Ultrasound-guided core biopsy performed. No family history of breast cancer. No prior breast procedures.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received: Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy, right breast, upper inner quadrant. 4 cores,
each 1.2-1.4 x 0.1 cm. Cores are grey-white and firm.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show a high-grade invasive carcinoma with solid growth pattern, marked nuclear pleomorphism,
and brisk mitotic activity (>20/10 HPF). Necrosis is present. The morphology is consistent with
invasive ductal carcinoma (IDC), NST, Nottingham Grade 3 (tubule score 3, nuclear score 3, mitotic
score 3; total 9/9). No in situ component is identified in the biopsy material.
Immunohistochemistry: ER -- equivocal result, 5% cells weakly positive (1+ intensity); result
borderline, recommend repeat testing or clinical correlation per ASCO/CAP guidelines.
PR: negative (0% cells). HER2: 3+ (positive by IHC; strong complete membrane staining in >10%
of tumor cells). FISH confirmation recommended per institutional protocol.
Ki-67: approximately 70%.

FINAL DIAGNOSIS:
Right breast, upper inner quadrant, core needle biopsy:
- Invasive ductal carcinoma, NST, Nottingham Grade 3
- ER: borderline/equivocal (5%, 1+) -- recommend repeat testing
- PR: negative
- HER2: positive (3+ IHC)
- Ki-67: ~70% (high proliferative index)
- Pathologic staging: cannot be determined on biopsy alone
- Note: Clinical staging, margins, lymph node status: not applicable on core biopsy specimen
