CLINICAL HISTORY:
58-year-old female with a palpable left breast mass, 2.3 cm on imaging. No prior breast surgery.
Family history of breast cancer (mother, age 62). Referred for modified radical mastectomy.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received: Left modified radical mastectomy specimen weighing 412 g. The breast measures
17.0 x 13.0 x 5.5 cm. On serial sectioning, a firm, stellate, grey-white mass measuring
2.3 x 2.1 x 1.8 cm is identified in the upper outer quadrant, 2.0 cm from the closest
(deep) margin. Axillary contents contain 18 lymph nodes, the largest measuring 1.5 cm.
No gross evidence of skin involvement or nipple retraction.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections from the primary mass show infiltrating ductal carcinoma (IDC), no special type (NST),
Nottingham Grade 2 (tubule formation 3, nuclear pleomorphism 2, mitotic count 1; score 6/9).
Lymphovascular invasion is identified within the tumor periphery. No perineural invasion is
seen. The deep surgical margin is 2.0 cm from the tumor; all other margins are negative.
Of the 18 axillary lymph nodes examined, 4 contain metastatic carcinoma (largest deposit 8 mm).
No evidence of extranodal extension. The overlying skin and nipple are unremarkable.
Immunohistochemistry: ER positive (Allred score 8/8, >95% cells, 3+ intensity). PR positive
(Allred score 6/8, 60% cells, 2+ intensity). HER2 score 1+ (negative by IHC).

FINAL DIAGNOSIS:
Left breast, modified radical mastectomy:
- Invasive ductal carcinoma, NST, Nottingham Grade 2
- Tumor size: 2.3 cm (greatest dimension)
- Lymphovascular invasion: present
- Perineural invasion: absent
- Surgical margins: negative (closest margin 2.0 cm, deep)
- Lymph nodes: 4/18 positive for metastatic carcinoma
- ER: positive | PR: positive | HER2: negative (1+ IHC)
- Pathologic stage: pT2 pN1a pM0 (AJCC 8th edition) -- Stage IIB
