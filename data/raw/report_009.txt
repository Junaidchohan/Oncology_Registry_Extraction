ago. PET scan: mildly FDG-avid (SUVmax 2.8), indeterminate. Radiologist comment: "suspicious for
malignancy, though inflammatory etiology cannot be excluded." Referred for CT-guided core biopsy.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received: CT-guided core needle biopsy, right lower lobe. 2 cores measuring 1.1 x 0.1 cm and
0.9 x 0.1 cm. Tissue is tan-grey and fragile.
