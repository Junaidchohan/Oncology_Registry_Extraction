CLINICAL HISTORY:
67-year-old male, colonoscopy revealed a 4.5 cm mass at the sigmoid colon. CT chest/abdomen/pelvis
showed no distant metastases. CEA: 8.2 ng/mL (elevated). Referred for sigmoid colectomy.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received: Sigmoid colectomy specimen, 28 cm in length. An ulcerated, fungating tumor measuring
4.5 x 3.8 x 1.2 cm is identified 8 cm from the distal resection margin and 22 cm from the
proximal margin. Tumor penetrates through the muscularis propria into the pericolorectal fat.
Regional mesentery contains 24 lymph nodes, ranging 0.3-2.1 cm. No evidence of perforation.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show moderately differentiated (Grade 2) colorectal adenocarcinoma. The tumor invades
through the full thickness of the muscularis propria into pericolorectal adipose tissue (pT3).
Lymphovascular invasion is present. Perineural invasion is identified in 2 foci. Of 24 lymph
nodes examined, 3 are positive for metastatic adenocarcinoma (pN1b). Proximal and distal
resection margins are free of tumor (proximal 22 cm, distal 8 cm). The circumferential radial
margin (CRM) is negative (closest point 2.5 mm). No distant metastatic disease was identified
in the specimen; CT correlation confirms no metastases.
Molecular: KRAS mutation detected (p.G12D, codon 12). NRAS wild-type. BRAF V600E: wild-type.
Microsatellite status: MSS (microsatellite stable) by IHC (MLH1+, MSH2+, MSH6+, PMS2+).
