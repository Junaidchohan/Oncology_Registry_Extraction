CLINICAL HISTORY:
52-year-old female, never-smoker, presenting with persistent cough and hemoptysis. CT chest revealed
a 3.1 cm spiculated right upper lobe mass with ipsilateral mediastinal lymphadenopathy. PET scan
showed FDG-avid lesion in the right upper lobe and multiple FDG-avid brain lesions (up to 1.2 cm).
Referred for CT-guided core needle biopsy of the lung mass.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Received: CT-guided core needle biopsy, right upper lobe, 3 cores each measuring approximately
1.5 x 0.1 cm. Cores are tan-grey and firm.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Sections show a malignant epithelial neoplasm arranged in lepidic, acinar, and papillary patterns
with focal mucin production, consistent with pulmonary adenocarcinoma. Tumor cells show moderate
nuclear pleomorphism, prominent nucleoli, and scattered mitoses. No squamous differentiation or
neuroendocrine features identified.
Immunohistochemistry: TTF-1 positive (diffuse, strong). Napsin-A positive. CK7 positive. CK20 negative.
p40 negative. Synaptophysin negative.
Molecular: EGFR exon 19 deletion detected (Del E746-A750). KRAS wild-type. ALK: negative by IHC.
ROS1: negative by IHC. PD-L1 TPS: 15% (22C3 assay). BRAF: not tested.
Clinical correlation: Brain lesions identified on PET/CT are consistent with metastatic disease.

FINAL DIAGNOSIS:
Right upper lobe, CT-guided core needle biopsy:
- Pulmonary adenocarcinoma, moderately differentiated
- EGFR exon 19 deletion positive
- KRAS wild-type | ALK negative | ROS1 negative
- PD-L1 TPS 15%
- Clinical stage: cT2a cN2 cM1b -- Stage IVA (brain metastases per PET/CT correlation)
- Note: ER, PR, HER2 not applicable for pulmonary adenocarcinoma
