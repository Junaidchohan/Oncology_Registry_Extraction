CLINICAL HISTORY:
49-year-old female with mammographically detected microcalcifications in the left breast (BI-RADS 5).
MRI revealed two distinct suspicious lesions: Lesion A -- 18 mm mass in the upper outer quadrant;
Lesion B -- 9 mm mass in the lower outer quadrant, 4 cm from Lesion A. Family history: sister with
BRCA1 mutation. Patient is BRCA1-positive (confirmed germline testing). Stereotactic core biopsy
performed of both lesions separately.

GROSS DESCRIPTION:
Specimen A (upper outer quadrant): 6 cores, 1.0-1.3 x 0.1 cm each. Grey-white and firm.
Specimen B (lower outer quadrant): 4 cores, 0.8-1.1 x 0.1 cm each. Grey-white.

MICROSCOPIC DESCRIPTION:
Specimen A (Upper Outer Quadrant):
Sections show invasive ductal carcinoma (IDC), NST, Nottingham Grade 3 (score 8/9). Associated
high-grade ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS), cribriform and solid subtypes with central necrosis.
Immunohistochemistry: ER positive (Allred 7/8, 70%, 3+ intensity). PR: equivocal (10%, 1+ intensity);
result borderline, recommend correlation with contralateral tumor. HER2: 2+ (equivocal by IHC);
FISH recommended. Ki-67: 55%.

Specimen B (Lower Outer Quadrant):
Sections show high-grade DCIS only (cribriform, solid, with necrosis). No invasive component
identified in submitted cores. Immunohistochemistry: ER positive (Allred 6/8, 50%, 2+ intensity).
PR positive (Allred 5/8, 30%, 2+ intensity). HER2: 1+ (negative).
Note: BRCA1 germline mutation is a known risk factor for triple-negative breast cancer; however,
neither lesion is triple-negative in this case. Consider genetic counseling correlation.

FINAL DIAGNOSIS:
Left breast, stereotactic core biopsy (two separate specimens):
Specimen A -- Upper outer quadrant (18 mm lesion):
  - Invasive ductal carcinoma, NST, Grade 3 (Nottingham 8/9)
  - Associated high-grade DCIS (cribriform/solid with necrosis)
  - ER: positive | PR: equivocal (recommend repeat) | HER2: equivocal (FISH recommended)
  - Ki-67: 55%

Specimen B -- Lower outer quadrant (9 mm lesion):
  - High-grade DCIS only; no invasive carcinoma identified
  - ER: positive | PR: positive | HER2: negative

Synchronous multifocal disease: present (two lesions, 4 cm apart)
BRCA1 germline mutation: positive (per clinical records)
Full staging: not applicable on core biopsy
